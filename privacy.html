Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Solace is designed to provide a private and supportive space for personal growth. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

1. Personal Data

Solace does not require an account, registration, or personal identification. We do not ask for your name, email address, or other personal details to use the app.

2. Journey Data

Your daily choices, journey history, and progress are stored locally on your device using Apple's data storage technologies.

Solace does not operate a server that receives or stores your journey records. Your journey data is not shared with the developer.

3. Analytics and Advertising

Solace does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not track your behavior for advertising purposes.

4. Optional In-App Purchases

Solace offers optional in-app purchases for users who wish to support its development. These purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase transactions according to its own privacy practices.

5. Data Control

Your journey information is managed locally by the app. Uninstalling Solace generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups or operating-system retention mechanisms.

6. Third-Party Services

Solace uses Apple's platform services for app distribution and optional in-app purchases. These services are subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies.

7. Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with website requests according to its own privacy practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Solace or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
